I'm a **Senior Engineer / Tech Lead** with **10 years of experience** building large-scale systems, working at the intersection of **distributed systems**, **cloud infrastructure**, and **real-time platform engineering**. At **Amazon Web Services**, I set the technical direction and architecture for real-time voice and ML inference systems that power [Amazon Connect](https://aws.amazon.com/connect/).

My work is the architecture behind **low-latency, highly available services at global scale** — multi-region systems built for serious concurrency and demanding production workloads. I lead the design of new platforms, the re-architecture of legacy ones, and the scaling, fault tolerance, and deployment foundations that keep them reliable. I operate across team boundaries, shaping long-term technical strategy and raising the engineering bar for how these systems are built.

Earlier in my career, I spent several years at Amazon Marketplace building large-scale, high-throughput seller systems on a service-oriented architecture.

I care about the hard parts of systems engineering — latency, reliability, cost, and the long-term architecture that lets platforms keep scaling. I'm also a **Hiring Bar Raiser** with **300+ interviews** under my belt, and I'm passionate about mentoring engineers and raising the hiring bar across teams.
